Kitchen
Rice Cooker
BALMUDA The Gohan · K08A-BK
  1. 1 Rinse rice and place in the inner pot.
  2. 2 Add water to the water line marked on the inner pot.
  3. 3 Important: Pour exactly 5cc of water into the outer steam chamber (the small port around the inner pot).
  4. 4 Close the lid and press the power button to start cooking.
  5. 5 Cooking takes approx. 25–30 minutes. Do not open the lid during cooking.
⚠ Never operate without water in the outer chamber — this will damage the appliance.

Bathroom
Hot Water & Bath Controller
NORITZ · RC-J101
  1. 1 Press 運転入/切 to turn the system on.
  2. 2 Press ふろ自動 (Auto Fill) to automatically fill the bathtub to the preset level and temperature.
  3. 3 Important: Auto Fill only fills to about 60% capacity. To fill to 90%, wait until the auto fill completes, then press 追いだき to continue heating and filling to your desired level.
  4. 4 Press 追いだき anytime to reheat the bath if the water has cooled.
  5. 5 Press 呼出 to call between the indoor and outdoor panels.
Current settings: Hot water 45°C · Bath 43°C.
If you prefer a cooler bath, please contact your host.
Bathroom · Indoor Panel
Bath Controller (Indoor)
NORITZ · RC-J101SE · Located inside the bathroom
  1. 1Same functions as the outdoor panel — use whichever is more convenient.
  2. 2Press ふろ自動 to start auto fill from inside the bathroom.
  3. 3Press 追いだき to reheat the bath.
  4. 4Press 呼出 to call the outdoor panel (e.g. to let someone know the bath is ready).
  5. 5Press 優先 to give priority control to this panel.
The indoor and outdoor panels control the same system. Changes made on one panel are reflected on the other.

Heating
Floor Heating
MEKAENJI Controller
  1. 1 Press 電源 (bottom right) to turn on.
  2. 2 Use ▼▲ 温度設定 to adjust temperature. Recommended: 28–30°C in winter.
  3. 3 Use エリア設定 ①②③④ to select which zones to heat. You can heat all zones or individual rooms.
  4. 4 Use タイマー予約 to schedule heating in advance.
  5. 5 Press 解除 to cancel a timer.
💡 Floor heating takes time to warm up — it is not instant like air conditioning.
Tip: Set the timer 1 hour before returning from skiing. The floor will be warm when you arrive.
